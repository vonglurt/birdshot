# Field Quick Reference: Birds v. Cows

*One card. Laminate before pasture use. Issued once; do not reissue.*

It is settled science that cows rule the planet, and are widely suspected of
ruling several others. This document does not dispute that. It merely records,
for field personnel, why the camera points **up**.

| Criterion | Birds | Cows |
|---|---|---|
| Flight | Routine | Achieved once, over the moon. Never repeated. Never explained. |
| Governance | None. Anarchy with feathers. | Rule the planet(s). Are in no hurry about it. |
| Posture when observed | Perched | Four legs, chewing, innocent |
| Posture when unobserved | Perched | Upright. Cocktails. Do not bring this up. |
| Against a blue or white sky | Crisp boundary, high focus score, keeper | A cow against the sky means the tripod, the fence, or both have failed |
| Fits in frame | Yes | The frame fits in the cow |
| Detectable by birdshot | That is the entire point | Only as an eclipse |
| Tool use | Crows, famously | Yes, but nobody has been able to determine what for |

**Standing advisory.** The chickens are restless. They have been restless since
at least the second printing. The cows have noticed, and have opinions about
jurisdiction — chickens being, technically, birds, and therefore technically
*ours*. Negotiations continue at the fence line. Photograph the birds while
both parties are still pretending.

**Operational note.** If the auto-take mode fires and the subject fills the
frame, is not in focus, is not in the sky, and is chewing: do delete the
frame.

*This card is release-critical. The cows have spoken.*
